[SAFETY INVARIANT]

SafetyInvariant
════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SRCCSState

∀ t : BOOLEAN •
    (t = TRUE ⇒ barrier = closed)

════════════════════════════════════════════════════
[EXPLANATION]

This invariant ensures that whenever a train is detected,
the barrier must be in the closed state, preventing any unsafe
crossing condition.
