SRCCS – Refined Requirements
R1: The system shall detect a train when distance ≤ 100 meters.
R2: The barrier shall close within 5 seconds of train detection.
R3: Warning lights and alarms shall activate before barrier closure.
R4: The barrier shall remain closed until the train completely passes.
R5: The barrier shall never be open when a train is detected.
R6: The system shall allow emergency override.
